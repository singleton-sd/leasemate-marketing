Features

Built around the lease, not a pile of documents.

These are the capabilities we are designing first for house owners and lease holders in Victoria.

Properties, rooms and beds

Set up each property as rooms and beds, so leads and contracts attach to a specific space.

Victorian contracts

Bed and room contracts for one or two occupants with a main contact, and whole-house leases on CAV Form 1 for up to six renters. You fill in the fields; the prescribed wording stays locked.

From lead to signed lease

Take a lead for a room or bed through inspection and deposit, then send the contract for signature by email. House managers and agents can prepare a contract; an owner or admin approves it before it goes out.

ID when someone becomes a tenant

Identity documents are collected only once a lead becomes a tenant, not during the enquiry.

Waitlist, not self-serve yet

Early access is a waitlist. There is no public signup into a live app from this site today.
