13. Data export and deletion on termination

  • If you close your customer account, we offer you an export of your Customer Data and start a 7-day grace period. You can cancel the closure during those 7 days.
  • When the grace period ends, we delete your Customer Data from LeaseMate’s live systems, including any owner summaries, except records that are on a legal hold (see below). Copies in our encrypted backups are removed as those backups are automatically deleted, which takes up to 35 days. Until then, backups are only used to restore the service, and we do not restore deleted customer or person data from them. Operational and system logs are kept for 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Legal holds. We keep signed tenancy agreements, bond lodgement records, and rent and holding-deposit payment records, encrypted and visible only to that customer’s owners and admins (not house managers). Until a Victorian tenancy lawyer and the Accountant confirm the required periods, these records are kept for 7 years from the end of the tenancy, then deleted. 30 days before the hold ends and these records are deleted, we prompt the customer’s owners and admins to export them. ID scans, applications and contact details are never placed on a legal hold and are deleted at once.
  • Owner summary (interim, pending lawyer review). After those held records are deleted, we keep a small summary of the tenancy for the customer: the property, the tenancy start and end dates, and the rent. It has no renter names, contact details or ID. Only that customer’s owners and admins can see it. It is kept until the customer’s account is deleted, and it is included in whole-customer deletion. We keep it because owners may need proof of rental periods for capital gains tax until 5 years after they sell the property. This retention is interim and awaits the lawyer’s review.
  • An owner or admin can delete one person’s information from the customer’s account after a two-step confirmation. That action is audited and takes effect straight away, subject to the legal holds above. Deleted information is not restored from backups.
  • ID documents also follow the deletion rules in our Privacy Policy (12 months after move-out, or 30 days after an application is rejected, then removed from backups the same way) and are never placed on a legal hold.
