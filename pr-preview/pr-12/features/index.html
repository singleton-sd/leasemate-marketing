Features

Built around the lease, not a pile of documents.

These are the capabilities we are designing first for house owners and lease holders in Victoria. Being built in Victoria. Not a live app yet.

Properties and rooms

Let a whole house, or let it room by room. Leads and agreements attach to the house or to a specific room.

Victorian contracts

Whole-house leases use the official CAV Form 1 for up to six renters. You fill in the allowed fields; the prescribed wording stays locked. Room sublets use a room agreement for one or two occupants.

From lead to agreement

Take a lead for a house or a room, then prepare the agreement. House managers and agents can prepare it; an owner or admin approves it before it goes out. Electronic signing is planned, not live yet.

ID before signing

Every occupant's ID is verified before the agreement can be sent for signing.

Bond lodging

LeaseMate tracks whether a bond has been marked as lodged and links out to the RTBA. Lodging happens on the RTBA site, not in the app.

Waitlist, not self-serve yet

Early access is a waitlist. There is no public signup into a live app from this site today.
