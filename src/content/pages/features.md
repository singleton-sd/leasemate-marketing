features:
  - title: Properties and rooms
    description: Let a whole house, or let it room by room. Leads and agreements attach to the house or to a specific room.
  - title: Victorian contracts
    description: "Whole-house leases use the official CAV Form 1 for up to six renters. You fill in the allowed fields; the prescribed wording stays locked. Room sublets use a room agreement for one or two occupants."
  - title: From lead to agreement
    description: Take a lead for a house or a room, then prepare the agreement. House managers and agents can prepare it; an owner or admin approves it before it goes out. Electronic signing is planned, not live yet.
  - title: ID before signing
    description: Every occupant's ID is verified before the agreement can be sent for signing.
  - title: Bond lodging
    description: LeaseMate tracks whether a bond has been marked as lodged and links out to the RTBA. Lodging happens on the RTBA site, not in the app.
  - title: Waitlist, not self-serve yet
    description: Early access is a waitlist. There is no public signup into a live app from this site today.
